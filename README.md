# Armed People Detection Dataset - 4,177 Videos
The dataset contains **4,177** videos across **1,045** scenes of a person carrying a **weapon** or similar object in different ways, captured simultaneously from four sides (front, right, back, left) on three iPhones, indoors and outdoors, in the morning, day, evening and night. It is designed for research in **gun violence** and **violence data**, focusing on various **detection** datasets and weapons detection methods, primarily aimed at **gun safety** and **public safety** applications. Specifically engineered to support **detection models**, **object detection**, and real-time gun violence incidents **monitoring systems**.

By utilizing this dataset, researchers and developers can advance their understanding and capabilities in **violence prevention**, **detections of guns and firearms**, and **public safety** technologies. - **[Get the data](https://unidata.pro/datasets/armed-people-detection/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=armed-people-detection)**

 Each scene features a person carrying a weapon or similar object, simulating potential threat scenarios for weapon detection systems. Annotations include weapon type (12 types), carry position (17 variants), location, and time of day.
